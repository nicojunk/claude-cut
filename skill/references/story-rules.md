# Story rules: hook, lock-in, benefit, end

From the user's feedback on the reference reels, 15.10.2026, and the Kallaway rules (kallaway-rules.md). Each rule says why.

## The order of a reel (30 to 70 s)

| Seconds | Part | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to ~4 | Hook | The strongest emotional sentence the creator says anywhere in the material, dark from frame 0 (saturation 0.35, brightness 0.7, slow zoom to 1.15, vignette). | the user 18:15: "die Hook muss krass sein, dann direkt Lock-in". An emotional, visual first sentence stops the thumb; the earlier benefit-first openers were weaker in his eyes. |
| hook end | Afterglow | At least 300 ms of room tone after the hook's last word before the flash and cut. | the user 23:17: "Bei dem 3. Clip wird zu früh der Clip gewechselt". The flash starts ~130 ms before the cut and landed on the last word. |
| ~4 to ~10 | Lock-in | The sentence right after the hook opens a question, a contrast or a guess; first dopamine hit here. | Kallaway: what you say directly after the hook decides if they stay; 80 % leave before a slow build gets interesting. |
| by 15 | Benefit | The creator's own sentence that says what the viewer gets, with an animation. | Kallaway's invisible timer (5, 15 or 30 s); the user 01:02: "da muss richtig hervorgehen, was da der Mehrwert ist". |
| middle | Explanation | Each hard term or end of a thought is a drop-off point: one card that shows the mechanism, in the creator's words. | the user 08.10. and 15.10.: animations exactly where viewers would leave, "nicht einfach willkürlich". |
| end | Close | A question or the call to action, never a flat statement; "mit" ends on the keyword card, "ohne" on the creator's own "Link in der Bio" sentence. | the user 01:02: "Das hört so abrupt auf". |

## The emotional core moment
Make it bigger with film language (slow zoom on the face, darker, desaturated, let the pause stand), never with a literal
picture (no sack, no hood, no dark room as B-roll). the user 15.10.2026 01:02: "Das muss richtig krass betont werden".

## Dopamine ladder (Kallaway, WluuZB_ck9Q)
A hit = an open question or gap (curiosity), anticipation of at least 0.7 s (the viewer guesses), the payoff on the exact
payoff word (card resolves, stamp, number lands), a soft sound at least 100 ms away from any cut and before the last
120 ms of a word. At least 2 per reel, spread: one in the lock-in, one in the middle or before the payoff.
Proven patterns from the reference reels: guess who is speaking (KI oder Mensch), 3-step build on "weil, weil, weil" with the
payoff on the last word, a roulette that stops on one of ten, a card that flips on the decisive word, a stamp on the
memorable sentence, a calendar that rolls and locks on the date.

## Choosing sentences
- Only the creator's recordings. Never write a sentence for them; the caption shows what they said (spelling fixed).
- Prefer takes that sound like talking to one person (Kallaway N09) and that show joy or conviction (N10).
- Pieces start and end in the silence between sentences. A seam inside a sentence is refused by the montage (it would
  change what the person says).
- Keep the creator's rhythm: pauses are capped (over 0.45 s down to 0.3 s), not removed; short and long sentences alternate.
- A slip of the tongue the creator then comments on stays (a slip in R3); a slip without comment may be replaced
  by a clean take of the same sentence.
- Back-references need their partner in the reel: "Woran liegt das?" needs the answer, "Ist das so?" needs the
  later "Wenn du sagst, das stimmt". Check with the order table before building.

## Two versions
"mit": call to action with the comment keyword, keyword card on the word "schreib"/"kommentiere". "ohne": the creator's own
sentence pointing to the link in the bio, "Link in der BIO" card. Both share hook, lock-in and middle.
