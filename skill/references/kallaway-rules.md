# Kallaway rules for short talking-head reels (research basis, never named in client texts)

Source: transcripts of the 10 newest long videos of @kallawaymarketing (14.10.2026) plus the earlier rule sets (04. and
07.10.2026). [id mm:ss] = start of the subtitle line. "Transfer" marks our application, not Kallaway's wording. Use these
as a model of where viewers leave; the drop-off map is labelled "Modell, keine gemessenen Absprünge".

## 1 Hook, first 0 to 5 seconds
- **N01** A talking mouth plus captions is not a visual hook. From second 0 a picture confirms the topic before the first
  sentence ends: show while you tell. "visual hooks are 10 times more effective than audio only hooks" [WluuZB_ck9Q 11:33].
  Transfer: the dark drama look and a card from frame 0 where the hook allows it.
- **N02** The first 1 to 2 seconds only stop the eye (colour, motion, brightness, contrast); processing takes ~200 ms
  [WluuZB_ck9Q 15:26].
- **N03** Anticipation hook: the picture shows something about to happen and comes closer [WluuZB_ck9Q 21:52].
- **B01** Own palette and motion language instead of feed patterns [WluuZB_ck9Q 15:56]. Transfer: channel profile.
- **B02** A slight zoom-in right at the start is enough motion [WluuZB_ck9Q 29:55]. Transfer: `start_zoom` 1.04 or the
  drama zoom (never both: pitfall 39).
- **B03** The hook = first sentence plus the first 2 to 3 seconds of picture; spoken, text and visual hook planned
  separately [vmsDhY9ITlI 0:56]. Transfer: cover text hook from hook-vorlagen.md.
- **B04** The first sentence names the topic, no vague teaser [WluuZB_ck9Q 11:20].
- **B05** If the picture raises no question, the text hook brings it in [WluuZB_ck9Q 30:30].

## 2 Lock-in and drop-off points
- **N04** No run-up: start at ~70 of 100 intensity; 80 % bounce before the rising action gets interesting [WluuZB_ck9Q 54:59].
- **N05** Order in the opening: what and why first, then who and how, place and time last. Transfer: never open on a
  picture that only shows the place or occasion.
- **N06** Invisible timer of 5, 15 or 30 seconds: by then the viewer knows what they get [WluuZB_ck9Q 60:30].
  Transfer: benefit by second 15 (gate in pruefe_dramaturgie.py).
- **B06** What you say directly after the hook is the real reason they stay [vmsDhY9ITlI 0:11]. Transfer: first dopamine hit.
- **B07** If the first picture is not instantly readable, say the same thing again in other words [vmsDhY9ITlI 20:18].

## 3 Rehooks
- **N07** Danger zone = where a thought ends and a new section starts; the same breath opens the next question, "There
  should never be a gap" [WluuZB_ck9Q 38:33]. Transfer: no dead air, no empty frame, no fade at a section change; a flash
  and chip or a new card instead.
- **B08** Rehook throughout the video to keep average view duration up [WluuZB_ck9Q 62:14].

## 4 Tempo and rhythm
- **N08** Alternate sentence lengths; monotone length makes them churn [WluuZB_ck9Q 6:26]. Transfer: cap pauses, do not
  equalise them (`pause_cap` 0.45 → 0.3 s).
- **N09** Film as if talking to one close friend [WluuZB_ck9Q 8:04]. Transfer: choose the take that sounds like a talk.
- **N10** Prefer takes with visible joy; it transfers through the lens [WluuZB_ck9Q 25:32].
- **B09** Delivery style (tone, rhythm, clear articulation) is a hard-to-copy asset [1ilMGCxJBQY 16:46].

## 5 B-roll, animation, explainers
- **N11** Say a hard point twice: plainly, then as a metaphor for a child [WluuZB_ck9Q 65:02]. Transfer: the explainer
  sits on the metaphor.
- **B11** Without a picture the viewer must imagine everything, that costs comprehension [WluuZB_ck9Q 65:31].
- **B12** Split screen with premium motion graphics as the fixed layout, 1 to 3 core layouts [S-7VkwSU0gs 20:56].
- **B13** For reach: raw emotion or maximum visual quality [94ezZMYPzls 17:17].
- **B17** It looks cheap when the layout execution is not premium [S-7VkwSU0gs 22:52].

## 6 Captions and text
No new rule in the 10 videos. Captions alone are no visual hook (N01); the text hook is designed apart from the captions.

## 7 Sound
- **B14** 4K and clean audio signal care [94ezZMYPzls 8:57]. Transfer: hiss fix, -14 LUFS, de-esser.
- **B15** The platform analyses picture, transcript and audio; music and sounds stay on topic [94ezZMYPzls 1:17].
  Transfer: no music without a licensed source; card sounds very soft (Nico 08.10.2026: "der Ton ist zu viel").

## 8 End, loop, call to action
- **N12** The last line is the most memorable and leads back into the first on replay [WluuZB_ck9Q 8:52]. Without a call
  to action the loop runs (reach); with one, conversion counts.
- **B16** The spoken call to action sits at the very end and offers the solution to the pain shown [gEdt3AGLWjI 7:53].

## 9 Premium look, face, set
- **B18** Faceless content rarely builds attachment [WluuZB_ck9Q 24:08]. Transfer: the speaker stays visible in split.
- **B19** Set and clothes show the profession [94ezZMYPzls 8:38].

## 10 AI editing
- **B20** One-shot editing templates are weak; edit after a reference reel [vwlK6MbdAto 44:39]. Transfer: this skill
  edits after the measured reference reels, and Nico's eye decides "premium".
